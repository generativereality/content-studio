# Hooks: proven templates, word for word, in four layers

A hook made up from scratch is a guess. The templates below are the creators' own words, as spoken or
shown in short videos that were saved as examples worth copying (read November 2026). Claims about
results are the creators', not checked. Use them as written: `[ ]` marks the blank you fill, and
nothing else in a template is reworded. Fill a template with the piece's own true facts; never an
invented number to make a template fit (SKILL rule 1).

**The person's own saves come first.** When the person keeps an index of the videos they saved or
admired (SKILL step 2), refresh this bank from it before choosing: read every saved piece about hooks,
copy each template word for word with its creator, and put theirs above the ones below. What they chose
to keep is the best guide to what they mean.

## The stack: four layers, one message

Every short video carries a hook in four layers in its first two seconds:

| Layer | What it is | Bank |
|---|---|---|
| **Say** | the first spoken line | §1 |
| **Text** | the words on screen | §2 |
| **Show** | the first picture or move | §3 |
| **Hear** | the music and the first sound | §4 |

The rules, from the saves:

- **Context in all of them at once.** *"You can say it in the audio … Show it in the visuals, write it
  in the text or caption, ideally all three, but the context must be established immediately."*
  (Kallaway)
- **A different proven hook per layer.** *"use three different proven viral hooks from all different
  videos in your one video for your verbal written and visual hook. It works wonders."* (Personal Brand
  Launch)
- **All saying the same thing.** *"What you say, what you show, and your text should all say the same
  thing."* (Kienobi)
- **Sound makes it hit.** *"A quiet music bed sets the mood, and a sound effect on each cut or reveal
  makes it hit. Keep the music low so it never fights your voice."* (Kienobi). *"Good audio can be
  really helpful but trending doesn't mean anything."* (Ella)
- **Rewrite the hook, not the topic.** *"When a reel stalls, don't throw out the topic. Rewrite the
  first 3 seconds."* (Kienobi)
- **Write many.** *"I write 50 hooks … Spread out your 50 hooks to cover all [levels of awareness]."*
  (Alex Hormozi)

**How to use it:** fill at least ten templates for the Say layer (different sources, different
shapes), choose one, then take the Text, Show and Hear layers from their own banks so that each is a
different template saying the same thing. Write the stack down as four lines on the script and the
storyboard's first row, each with its template's number. For a post or a page, only the Say and Text
banks apply; the same rule holds: a template filled, never a hook invented.

## 1 · Say (spoken hooks)

**Joshua Aboukhater, "10 hooks that always work"**
1. Nobody mentions this.
2. I wish I knew this earlier.
3. Pause for a second.
4. Ever notice this pattern?
5. Here's the real truth.
6. Let me save you hours.
7. This may surprise you.
8. You need this now.
9. You may not agree with this.
10. I just figured this out.

**Sofi Pasos, four curiosity templates**
11. [Name a popular belief], then contradict it. *"people will stay for to understand why"*
12. "[X] will not work unless you do the following." *"people will stay for to discover what they need to do"*
13. Show first the result, then how you did it. *"people will stay for to learn the step"*
14. "It is [A], not [B], in reality it is [C]." *"people will stay for to discover what is C"*

**Kallaway, five attention triggers** (for the hook or the line right after it)
15. Extreme size and speed: "Here's how I [big result] in just [short time]." (*"Here's how I plan 60
    days of content in just 30 minutes."*)
16. Specific numbers or dollars: *"I spent $342,000 on private jets last year. Here's exactly where I
    flew and how much it cost."*
17. A name the viewer wants to be like: *"[Name] just went from zero to $10 million per year in just
    three years. Here's how she did it."*
18. Little experience, huge result: *"Here's how to get your first million view video in your first
    30 days of posting."*
19. A graph going up and to the right (shown, not said).

**The triple hook: context, pull, whiplash** (Kallaway; Iamaayushswamy). Line one says what it is
about; then a pull; then the contrarian snapback. *"A great hook isn't catchy. It's a trap. Context
sets it in, the pull loads it, and then the whiplash shuts it."* The four pulls (Iamaayushswamy):
20. *Taboo pull:* "Say something that feels slightly uncomfortable or socially dangerous to say out loud."
21. *Dark pull:* "Expose something hidden that the viewer didn't know was actually happening to them."
22. *Contradiction pull:* "Say something that directly contradicts what they already believe to be true."
23. *Proof pull:* "Give factual evidence or stats to back up what you're already saying."
24. Context, example: *"This is not an inclined chest press."*
25. Whiplash, example: *"This reel got 2 million views and made zero sales. Here's what I'm doing to
    never go viral again."*

**Kallaway, the desire hook** (for selling)
26. Identity call-out + desire frame + proof anchor: "This is how you [desire] if you're [identity]."
    (*"This is how you teach your kids how to deal with money if you're a rich parent."*), proof right after.

**Personal Brand Launch, "unskippable": context, challenge, transition** (opens a series)
27. "[Context], so today's day one of [challenge]. But let me start from the beginning." (*"I can't
    afford to propose to my girlfriend, so today's day one of door dashing for a diamond ring. But let
    me start from the beginning."*)
28. *"This is day one of getting my 24-7 cafe out of this much debt. My study cafe is going bankrupt,
    and I'm here to find out why."*
29. *"In 30 days, my dad loses his company for doing the right thing …"*

**Psychology of Ads, the mirror method**
30. "If you're [the moment], but [the stakes]. Here's exactly what I [the loop]." (*"If you're
    exhausted after work, but can't justify another $30 on Uber Eats. Here's exactly what I keep in my
    fridge."*) To fill it: *"My customer is thinking A, but what they really care about is B, and
    they'll keep watching because of C."*

**Psychology of Ads, the story loop**
31. Stakes: *"…went from Oprah's favorite things to $400,000 worth in debt, and now you have less than
    30 days to save it."*
32. Question: *"What if you could turn your two biggest obsessions into a career?"*
33. Pattern interrupt: *"So on May, 2020, everything changes."*
34. Re-hook: *"So we're documenting all of it. The wins, the mistakes, the lessons."*

**Kienobi, one topic, six hooks, each beating the last** (the ladder from weak to strong; the topic in
brackets is the blank)
35. "How to [go viral on Instagram]."
36. "How to [go viral on Instagram] in 60 seconds."
37. "[Posting every day] won't make you [go viral], this will, in 60 seconds."
38. "I broke down every [reel that pulled 5 times more than creator's average view], and they all had
    one thing in common."
39. "Most [creators] think [going viral] is all about [luck], but it's not even about [the content],
    it's about [the first 3 seconds]."
40. "Is it possible to go from [0 followers] to [100,000 followers] in [30 days]?"
*"Weak hooks tell you what the video is about. Strong ones tell you you've had it wrong. Story hooks
leave you with a question you need answered."*

**Others**
41. "If you want to reduce your skip rate, open your content with the result or the tension."
    (Executive Coach Authority: the rule as its own hook)
42. "I'm going to show you exactly why [everybody has an AI idea and almost nobody makes money from
    it], and the one system that fixes it." (Professor Glitch)
43. "You lose money every time you [toss a receipt]." (Professor Glitch)
44. "Never post another [reel] without fixing this." (Kienobi)
45. "Don't try [a pike press] until you can do these [5] things." (a teaching reel)
46. "I'm [53]. Here's what I wish I'd known at [35]." (a teaching reel)
47. "Number [3] is the one everyone gets wrong." (a teaching reel)
48. "Here's how to get your videos past these view ceilings." (Kallaway)
49. "These are the only three things you need in [your hook] if you want [your videos to drive more
    sales]." (Kallaway)
50. "This is everything I don't do when I'm [creating content] as [a social media strategist who's
    grown over 14,000 followers in the last 30 days]." (The Wellness Socialite)

Check the chosen line against **the four S's** (Kallaway): *"Subject, it's clear what the video's
about. Stakes, there's a clear reason why the viewer should keep watching. Simple, all the words are
third grade language … super clear. All the sentences are short, punchy."*

## 2 · Text (the written hook on screen)

T1. "Which Level Are You At? 👇" (Kienobi)
T2. "[40] [Visual Hooks] To Use in Your Content." (Personal Brand Launch)
T3. "Never post another [reel] without fixing this" (Kienobi: the text and the voice are the same line)
T4. "USE THESE [SONGS]" + one category word under it (Viewcci)
T5. "[Thing] ✅" as a fixed title (Blake Armitage, *"BMU Pull ✅"*)
T6. "[Skill] in 60 Seconds" (*"Handstand Progressions in 60 Seconds"*)

Any line from §1 works as the text, as long as it is a **different** template from the one spoken and
says the same thing. The person's niche's own outlier titles (SKILL step 2) go here too, word for word,
with the channel and how far they beat it.

whiteboard · whiteboard · movement · PNG arrow with label · mirror · the walk-in slash walk-up · a bunch

Also: *"if you can visually show [the pull], the stronger your pull will be"* (Iamaayushswamy);
*"graphics and charts visually going up and to the right"* (Kallaway); *"If you mention a number or an
app, put it on screen"* (Kienobi).

## 4 · Hear (audio hooks)

**Music by kind of video.** Viewcci, "Use these songs" groups tracks by what the video is: storytelling,
informational, challenge, day in the life, motivational, emotional. Examples of each kind as titled on
screen: storytelling *Way Down We Go*, *Runaway*; informational *Timeless*, *Sprinter*; challenge *He's
a Pirate*, *Nightcall*; day in the life *Paper Planes*, *Space Cadet*; motivational *Flashing Lights*,
*Dragonfly*; emotional *See You Again*. Choose the kind first, then a track of that kind.

**Licence:** these are commercial tracks. On Instagram, Facebook and TikTok use them only from the app's
own music library when posting; never baked into the file. On YouTube long-form, not at all: use a track
of the same mood from YouTube's Audio Library or one made for the piece (`audio.md`,
`costs-and-licences.md`).

**Sound effects.** *"a sound effect on each cut or reveal makes it hit."* (Kienobi). Free sources named
in a saved video (Thomas Smith, read November 2026): *"#1 Openverse: 5,223,435 free audio files, one
API, no key #2 Real SFX by Clipwing: real recordings, placed on the exact frame #3 earshot by Leon Lin:
new sounds made in code, even a whole kit."* Check each one's licence per sound before use.
